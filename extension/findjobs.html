What you're looking for

Saved automatically. Up to 3 roles go into each search.

A routine that finds more openings
  1. Every morning, set Past 24 hours and open the searches. Applying in the first day or two gets you seen far more often.
  2. Open the company career site searches too: the same jobs often aren't on LinkedIn, and fewer people apply.
  3. On a job you like, click the extension and Save job. It goes into My applications → To apply, so nothing gets lost.
  4. Set up job alerts on LinkedIn and Naukri with the same roles, so new jobs come to you.
